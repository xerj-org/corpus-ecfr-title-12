Title 12 CFR — Part 19—RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
Subpart A—Uniform Rules of Practice and Procedure
## § 19.4 Authority of the Comptroller.

§ 19.4 Authority of the Comptroller.

The Comptroller may, at any time during the pendency of a proceeding, perform, direct the performance of, or waive performance of, any act which could be done or ordered by the ALJ.
