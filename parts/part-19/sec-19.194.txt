Title 12 CFR — Part 19—RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
Subpart K—Parties and Representational Practice Before the OCC; Standards of Conduct
## § 19.194 Eligibility of attorneys and accountants to practice.

§ 19.194 Eligibility of attorneys and accountants to practice.

(a) Attorneys. Any attorney not currently under suspension or debarment pursuant to this subpart may practice before the OCC.

(b) Accountants. Any accountant not currently under suspension or debarment by the OCC may practice before the OCC.
