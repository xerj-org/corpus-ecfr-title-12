Title 12 CFR — Part 1240—CAPITAL ADEQUACY OF ENTERPRISES
Subpart D—Risk-Weighted Assets—Standardized Approach
## § 1240.61 Purpose and scope.

§ 1240.61 Purpose and scope.

Sections 1240.61 through 1240.63 of this subpart establish public disclosure requirements related to the capital requirements and buffers described in subpart B and subpart G.
