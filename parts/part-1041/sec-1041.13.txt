Title 12 CFR — Part 1041—PAYDAY, VEHICLE TITLE, AND CERTAIN HIGH-COST INSTALLMENT LOANS
Subpart D—Recordkeeping, Anti-Evasion, Severability, and Dates
## § 1041.13 Prohibition against evasion.

§ 1041.13 Prohibition against evasion.

A lender must not take any action with the intent of evading the requirements of this part.
