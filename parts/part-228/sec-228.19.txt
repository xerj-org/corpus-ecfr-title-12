Subpart B—Geographic Considerations
## § 228.19 Areas for eligible community development loans, community development investments, and community development services.

§ 228.19 Areas for eligible community development loans, community development investments, and community development services.

The Board may consider a bank's community development loans, community development investments, and community development services provided outside of its facility-based assessment areas, as provided in this part.
