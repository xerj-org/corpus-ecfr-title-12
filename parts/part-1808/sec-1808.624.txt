Subpart F—Terms and Conditions of Guarantee
## § 1808.624 Criminal provisions.

§ 1808.624 Criminal provisions.

The criminal provisions of 18 U.S.C. 657 regarding embezzlement or misappropriation of funds are applicable to all CDFI Bond Guarantee Program participants and insiders.
