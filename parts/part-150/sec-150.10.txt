Title 12 CFR — Part 150—FIDUCIARY POWERS OF FEDERAL SAVINGS ASSOCIATIONS
## § 150.10 What regulations govern the fiduciary operations of Federal savings associations?

§ 150.10 What regulations govern the fiduciary operations of Federal savings associations?

A Federal savings association (“you”) must conduct its fiduciary operations in accordance with 12 U.S.C. 1464(n) and this part.
