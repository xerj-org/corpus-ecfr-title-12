Title 12 CFR — Part 270—OPEN MARKET OPERATIONS OF FEDERAL RESERVE BANKS
Regulations Relating to Open Market Operations of Federal Reserve Banks
## § 270.1 Authority.

§ 270.1 Authority.

This part is issued by the Federal Open Market Committee (the “Committee”) pursuant to authority conferred upon it by sections 12A and 14 of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 263, 355).
